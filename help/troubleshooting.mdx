---
title: "Troubleshooting"
description: "Quick fixes for common problems."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't sign in">
    Check your details for typos. Make sure you have internet. If it still fails, ask your admin to reset your password.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't see my tasks">
    Pull down to refresh. If nothing appears, you may not have been assigned work yet. Ask your supervisor.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My form won't submit">
    Check that every required question is answered. Then check your internet connection and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The app is slow or frozen">
    Close it fully and open it again. Make sure you have the latest version from the Play Store.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My form was sent back">
    Open it, read the note from your supervisor, fix the problem and submit again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm using an iPhone">
    The iOS app is coming soon. Use the [web version](https://advadproject.zivahtechnologies.com/) for now.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
