## What is a project?

A **project** is one enumeration exercise, for example "Appliance count for Community A".

## What is a task?

A **task** is a piece of that work given to you, such as a household or building to visit.

## Find your tasks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Go to your home screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a project">
    Tap the project you are working on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a task">
    Tap a task to open it and start recording.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Finished tasks are marked as done, so you always know what is left.
</Tip>
