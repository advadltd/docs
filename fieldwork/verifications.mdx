---
title: "Verifications"
description: "Double-checking that records are true."
---

A **verification** is a second check to confirm the information collected is accurate, for example revisiting a location to confirm the appliances recorded.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="A record is picked for checking">
    [CONFIRM: random or chosen by supervisor?]
  </Step>
  <Step title="The checker visits or reviews it">
    They compare what was recorded with what they find.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Result is saved">
    The record is marked as confirmed, or flagged for correction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Verifications protect the quality of the whole project, so please treat them as helpful, not as a punishment.
</Tip>
