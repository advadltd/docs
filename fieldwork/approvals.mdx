---
title: "Approvals"
description: "How submitted forms are checked and accepted."
---

Every submitted form is reviewed to make sure the information is complete and correct.

## Possible outcomes

| Status | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Submitted** | Waiting for review | Nothing, just wait |
| **Approved** | Accepted | You are done |
