## What is an SOS?

An SOS is an emergency alert you send from the app when you need help. Your supervisors and administrators are notified so they can act quickly.

## Send an SOS (Enumerators)

### When to use it

Send an SOS if you:

- Feel unsafe
- Are hurt or unwell
- Are threatened or harassed
- Face any serious problem while working

<Tip>
  If you are unsure whether it is serious enough, send it anyway. It is always better to ask for help early.
</Tip>

### After you send it

- Stay calm and move to a safe place if you can.
- Keep your phone with you and switched on.
- Your team will contact you.

### Good practice for responders

- Respond **immediately**. Do not wait until later.
- Always **record what happened**, even if the situation ends quickly.
- If the person cannot be reached, follow your organisation's emergency plan.

<Warning>
  Treat every SOS as real until you have spoken to the person and confirmed they are safe.
</Warning>
