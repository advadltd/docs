Enumerators can collect and submit records from the mobile app or the web application. Choose your platform below for the complete process.

    ## Before you begin

    Sign in and connect to the internet before your first field visit. The app downloads your assigned projects, tasks, and form definitions so they are available when you lose connectivity.

    <Warning>
      A form must have been downloaded to the phone before it can be opened offline. If the app asks you to connect, go online once and reopen the task.
    </Warning>

    ## Save and resume a draft

    Your answers are autosaved on the phone while you work. If you leave an unfinished form, choose one of these options:

    - **Continue editing** returns to the form.
    - **Save draft & exit** keeps the answers on this phone so you can continue later.
    - **Discard draft** permanently removes the unfinished local copy.

    <img
      src="/images/image-10.png"

    />

    To continue a saved form, open **My Submissions**, find the record marked **Resume**, and tap it. You can also resume the current draft from its card on **Home**. Review any previously entered answers, finish the remaining sections, and submit normally.

    <Note>
      An unfinished draft has not been submitted. It remains on the device until you finish and submit it, sync it successfully, or delete it.
    </Note>

    ## Work offline

    You can fill forms without internet access after the assigned task and its form have been downloaded to the phone.

    While offline:

    - answers continue to autosave locally;
    - drafts can be closed and resumed on the same phone;
    - location and evidence photos can be added when the form requests them; and
    - tapping **Submit** completes the form locally and places it in the sync queue.

    A completed offline record is shown as **To Sync** or **Sync now**. It is safe on the phone, but it has not reached the server yet. When connectivity returns, allow the app to sync. If manual sync is available, open **My Submissions** and tap **Sync now**, or use **Sync All** to send every waiting record.

    Keep the app installed and do not clear its data while records are waiting to sync. Evidence photos may take longer than answers to upload, especially on a slow connection.

    <Tip>
      Before leaving an area with connectivity, open the projects and tasks you expect to use. After fieldwork, check the **To Sync** tab before signing out or changing devices.
    </Tip>

    ## See your drafts and submissions

    Open **My Submissions** from **Home** or the app navigation. The list combines records saved on the phone with records already received by the server.

    - **All** shows drafts, records waiting to sync, and synced submissions.
    - **To Sync** shows completed records that still need to reach the server.
    - **Synced** shows records successfully received by the server.
    - **Resume** identifies an unfinished draft; tap it to continue the form.
    - **Sync now** identifies a completed record awaiting upload; tap it to retry.

    <img

    Tap any record to see its answers, evidence, reference, dates, and current status. Pull down on the list to refresh it when you are online.

    <Warning>
      Delete a draft only when you are certain it is no longer needed. Deleting an unsynced draft removes that copy from the phone.
    </Warning>

    ## If a submission does not sync

    1. Confirm that the phone has a working internet connection.
    2. Open **My Submissions** and select **To Sync**.
    3. Tap **Sync now** on the record, or **Sync All** if several records are waiting.
    4. Keep the app open while evidence photos upload.
    5. If the record shows **Action required**, open its details and read the server message before trying again or contacting your supervisor.

    ## Complete and submit a form

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Open the web application in a supported browser and sign in with your enumerator account.

      <Step title="Open your assignment">
        Go to your assigned projects or tasks, select the correct project, and open the task you want to complete.

      <Step title="Start the form">

    ## Drafts on the web

    If the web form offers **Save draft**, use it before leaving an incomplete form. Return to your drafts or submissions list and select the draft to continue it. A draft is not a completed submission and will not enter the review process until you finish the form and select **Submit**.

    <Warning>
      The web application requires an internet connection. Do not rely on an open browser tab as an offline copy, and do not close or refresh the page while a save or submission is in progress.
    </Warning>

    ## See your submissions

    Open **Submissions** or **My Submissions** in the web navigation. Use the available status filters to find drafts, submitted records, or records that have progressed through review. Select a record to inspect its answers, evidence, reference number, submission date, and status.

    If a newly submitted record is not visible, refresh the list once. If the form never displayed a success confirmation, reopen the draft or contact your supervisor before entering the same record again.
  </Tab>

## What happens after submission

Once a synced record reaches the server, an authorized supervisor can review it. Its status may change as it moves through the review and verification process. See [Approvals](/fieldwork/approvals) for the next stage.

<Info>
  Saving a draft, completing a form, and syncing it are different steps. A record is fully submitted only when it appears as **Synced** on mobile or the web application confirms receipt.
</Info>
