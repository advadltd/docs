## Fill in a form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a task">
    Tap the task, then tap **Start** or **Fill form**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer each question">
    Examples: appliance type, how many, whether it works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add more appliances">
    Repeat for each appliance you find.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Check your answers before sending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Tap **Submit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Working without internet

[CONFIRM: does the app save forms offline and send later?] If it does, you can fill in forms with no signal. They will send automatically once you are back online.

<Note>
  Questions marked as required must be answered before you can submit.
</Note>

## After you submit

Your supervisor will review your form. See [Approvals](/fieldwork/approvals).
