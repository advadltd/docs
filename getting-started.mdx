## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your login details">
    Your Supervisor or administrator will give you your login details with your ID and Password. 
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Type them in on the sign-in screen and tap **Sign in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look around">
    You will land on your home screen. What you see depends on your [role](/roles/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep your login details private. Never share your password, even with colleagues.
</Tip>
