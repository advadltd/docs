## What is Advad?

Advad is a secure field operations app for **enumerators, supervisors and administrators**. It helps teams collect accurate information about electrical appliances, wherever the work takes them.

With Advad, enumerators can:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Open assigned projects" icon="folder-open">
    See the work that has been given to you.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Complete structured forms" icon="clipboard-list">
    Answer clear questions, so every record looks the same.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Capture location and photos" icon="location-dot">
    Record GPS coordinates and add supporting photos.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Save drafts" icon="floppy-disk">
    Stop halfway and finish later, without losing your work.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Work offline" icon="wifi-slash">
    Keep collecting data with no internet. Send your records when you are back online.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Offline work is available on the **Android and iPhone apps**. The web version needs an internet connection.
</Note>
