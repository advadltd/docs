- How many forms are submitted
- Open SOS Incidents not yet resolved
- Total approved submissions

## Exports

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Exports">
    Go to the export section by clicking on the **filter and export records **
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what you need">
    Choose the project and date range.

    You can make use of Filters to pick the submissions you wish to export

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-24-at-13.26.20.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2026 10 24 At 13 26 20"
      title="Screenshot 2026 10 24 At 13 26 20"
      style={{ width:"75%" }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Use the Export Excel button to download the file.

    ![Screenshot 2026 10 24 At 13 29 13](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-24-at-13.29.13.png "Screenshot 2026 10 24 At 13 29 13")

    From your Download History, click on the file to open in Excel or Google Sheets.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-24-at-13.30.56.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2026 10 24 At 13 30 56"
      title="Screenshot 2026 10 24 At 13 30 56"
      style={{ width:"70%" }}
    />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Exported files contain real people's information. Store them safely and share only with those who need them.
</Warning>
