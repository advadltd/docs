---
title: "Project Builder"
description: "Create a project and the form your team will use."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    Give it a clear name and short description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the form">
    Add questions such as appliance type, quantity, condition and power rating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose question types">
    For example: text, number, choose-from-a-list, photo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark required questions">
    So nothing important is skipped.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and publish">
    Your team can now see it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep forms short. Fewer questions means faster work and fewer mistakes.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Changing a form after people start using it may affect work already done. Make changes early.
</Note>
