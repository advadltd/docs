---
title: "Assignments"
description: "Decide who does which work."
---

An **assignment** links people to the Projects and tasks they are responsible for.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a project">
    Open the project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select tasks or areas">
    Pick the work to hand out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the person">
    Select an enumerator, and a supervisor to oversee them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The person will see the work in their app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Spread work evenly, and keep each person's areas close together to save travel time.
</Tip>
