Use **Team** to manage the people who can sign in to the app. Each person should have their own account so their work and actions can be identified correctly.

<Note>
  Admins can manage Supervisor and Enumerator accounts. Only the Super Admin can create or fully manage Admin accounts. No user can create another Super Admin.
</Note>

## Understand the roles

| Role | Main purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Enumerator** | Collects records for assigned tasks and sees their own submissions |
| **Supervisor** | Oversees assigned Projects, approves Enumerator records, and responds to team SOS alerts |
| **Admin** | Manages Projects, forms, people, assignments, records, and operations globally |
| **Super Admin** | Has all Admin abilities plus system-owner actions such as creating Admins and archiving |

## Create an account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team">
    Select **Team** from the main navigation, then select **Add account**.

    ![Image](/images/image-13.png "Image")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the account ID">
    Use a unique ID the person will remember. This is their sign-in ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the person's details">
    Add the full name, a temporary password of at least eight characters, and an optional district.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the role">
    Select **Enumerator** or **Supervisor**. A Super Admin can also select **Admin**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a direct Supervisor">
    For an Enumerator, select the active Supervisor responsible for their work. This is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and hand over the account">
    Select **Create account**. Share the account ID and temporary password privately with the user.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never create shared accounts. Do not send passwords in public groups or include them in reports.
</Warning>

## Reassign an Enumerator

Use reassignment when an Enumerator moves to a different Supervisor.

1. Find the Enumerator in the team table.
2. Choose the new person in **Supervisor / transition**.
   ![Screenshot 2026 10 25 At 21 42 06](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-25-at-21.42.06.png "Screenshot 2026 10 25 At 21 42 06")
3. Select **Reassign**.
4. Wait for the success message and confirm the new Supervisor is displayed.

## Promote an Enumerator

1. Find an active Enumerator.
2. Select **Promote**.
3. Confirm the change to Supervisor.
4. Review their Project assignments and reporting responsibilities.

Promotion changes the person's role but does not automatically give them new Project assignments.

## Demote a Supervisor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reassign direct reports">
    Move every active Enumerator who reports to this Supervisor to another active Supervisor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the person's new Supervisor">
    In **Supervisor / transition**, select who will supervise the person after demotion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Demote the account">
    Select **Demote** and confirm. The account becomes an Enumerator account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Demotion is blocked while the Supervisor still has active direct reports.
</Note>

## Deactivate or reactivate an account

Use **Deactivate** when access should stop temporarily. The person can no longer sign in, but their records and history remain. Select **Reactivate** later to restore access.

Before deactivation, review the person's active assignments and operational responsibilities. You cannot deactivate your own account or the Super Admin account.

## Delete an account

Deleting permanently disables sign-in for that account. Submitted records and photos remain and are marked as belonging to a deleted account.

1. Confirm that permanent removal is the correct decision.
2. Reassign any work or direct reports first.
3. Select **Delete account** and carefully read the confirmation.
4. Confirm the deletion.

<Warning>
  Account deletion cannot be reversed. Prefer **Deactivate** when the person may return.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot create an Admin">
    Only the Super Admin can create an Admin account. A regular Admin can create Supervisors and Enumerators.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot create an Enumerator">
    Make sure at least one active Supervisor exists and is selected as the Enumerator's direct Supervisor.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A Supervisor cannot be demoted">
    Reassign all active direct reports first, then select the Supervisor who will manage the demoted user.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An action button is not shown">
    The action may not be allowed for your role, for your own account, for the Super Admin, or for the account's current status.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
