## Add a person

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team">
    Go to the team section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add new member">
    Enter their name and contact details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">
    Enumerator, Supervisor or Admin. See [Roles](/roles/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the login details">
    Share them privately with the person.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Other things you can do

- Change someone's role.
- Deactivate a person who has left the project.
- Reset a forgotten password. [CONFIRM]
