You prepare everything the field team needs, then keep an eye on progress.

## What you can do

- Create projects and forms with the [Project builder](/administration/project-builder).
- Add people and manage the team in [Team management](/administration/team-management).
- Give work to people in [Assignments](/administration/assignments).
- View progress and download reports in [Analytics and exports](/administration/analytics-and-exports).

## Setting up a new project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the project and form">
    Decide what information to collect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team">
    Invite supervisors and enumerators.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the work">
    Share places or tasks among your enumerators.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track progress">
    Check the dashboard regularly.
  </Step>
</Steps>
