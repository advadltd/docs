Everyone in the app has a role. Your role decides what you can see and do.

## Choose your role

Everyone who uses Advad has a role. Your role decides which screens and actions you can see.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Enumerator" icon="person-walking" href="/roles/enumerator">
    **Visits places and records the appliances found.**

    - Open assigned projects
    - Fill in forms, add photos and GPS
    - Save drafts and work offline
    - Submit completed records
  </Card>

  <Card title="Supervisor" icon="user-check" href="/roles/supervisor">
    **Checks the work of a group of enumerators and keeps them on track.**

    - See your team's progress
    - Review and approve submissions
    - Send back records that need fixing
    - Respond to SOS alerts
  </Card>

  <Card title="Admin" icon="user-gear" href="/roles/admin">
    **Sets up projects, forms and teams.**

    - Build projects and forms
    - Add and manage team members
    - Assign work
    - View reports and download data
  </Card>

  <Card title="Super Admin" icon="user-shield" href="/roles/super-admin">
    **Looks after the whole system and everyone in it.**

    - Everything an Admin can do
    - Manage admins
    - See activity across all projects
    - Control system-wide settings
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Which guide should I read?

| If you... | Read |
| --- | --- |
| Visit locations and collect data | [Enumerator guide](/roles/enumerator) |
| Check and approve other people's work | [Supervisor guide](/roles/supervisor) |
| Set up projects and manage teams | [Admin guide](/roles/admin) |
| Oversee the entire system | [Super Admin guide](/roles/super-admin) |

<Tip>
  Not sure which role you have? Ask the person who gave you your login details. If your role needs to change, an Admin can update it for you.
</Tip>
