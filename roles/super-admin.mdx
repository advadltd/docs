You are the person on the ground. Your job is to visit locations and record the appliances you find.

## Your day in 4 steps

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile app (Android & iPhone)">
    <Note>
      The mobile app works **offline**. You can fill in forms with no internet and they will send once your phone is back online.
    </Note>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Open the app and sign in (see `Sign In Guide` ). Do this once while you have internet, before heading out.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check your tasks">
        See the Projects assigned to you form the Home Tab. [Learn more](/fieldwork/projects-and-tasks).

        <img
          src="/images/Screenshot_1792830088.png"
          alt="Screenshot 1792830088"
          title="Screenshot 1792830088"
          style={{ width:"24%" }}
        />

        By Clicking the View tasks Button, you are redirected with a page listing out all the tasks in that project assigned to you by your supervisor
      </Step>
      <Step title="Visit and fill in the form">
        Carry out the tasks assigned to you. You can do this even with no Internet Connection. [Learn more](/fieldwork/forms-and-submissions).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Submit">
        Tap **Submit**. If you are offline, your form is saved on your phone and sent automatically when you reconnect.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      Before you leave for the field, open the app with internet so your tasks are loaded. When you are back in a network area, keep the app open for a moment so your forms can finish sending.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Web version">
    <Warning>
      The web version needs an **internet connection** the whole time. If your connection drops, you may lose what you have not yet submitted.
    </Warning>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign in">
        Go to [advadproject.zivahtechnologies.com](https://advadproject.zivahtechnologies.com/) and sign in.

        On successful sign in, you are given this overview of your Enumeration activity

        <img
          src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-24-at-09.25.39.png"
          alt="Screenshot 2026 10 24 At 09 25 39"
          title="Screenshot 2026 10 24 At 09 25 39"
          style={{ width:"71%" }}
        />
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check your tasks">
        Navigate using the Sidebar on the Left to Projects to see the Projects assigned to you. [Learn more](/fieldwork/projects-and-tasks).

        <img
          src="/images/image.png"
          alt="Image"
          title="Image"
          style={{ width:"61%" }}
        />

        Use the Open Project button to see all tasks under that Project

        <img
          src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-24-at-09.31.41.png"
          alt="Screenshot 2026 10 24 At 09 31 41"
          title="Screenshot 2026 10 24 At 09 31 41"
          style={{ width:"67%" }}
        />
      </Step>
      <Step title="Visit and fill in the form">
        For each task you want to record, click on the Collect button for that tasks which opens up the form. [Learn more](/fieldwork/forms-and-submissions).

        <img
          src="/images/Screenshot-2026-10-24-at-09.37.32.png"

      </Step>
      <Step title="Submit">
        Web Version allows us to save a Submission as draft by using the **Save draft** button meaning an enumerator can edit the submission later before final submission. By Taping **Submit Record ** this indicates final submission and cannot be edited again and would proceed to approval by the supervision.

        <Note>
          Ensure to save your tasks, so nothing is lost
        </Note>

        ![Screenshot 2026 10 24 At 09 39 31](/images/Screenshot-2026-10-24-at-09.39.31.png "Screenshot 2026 10 24 At 09 39 31")
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      If you will be working in an area with weak or no network, use the mobile app instead.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Good habits

- Charge your phone before heading out.
- Fill in every required question.
- Submit often so nothing is lost.

<Warning>
  If you are ever in danger, use the SOS button right away. See [SOS incidents](/fieldwork/sos-incidents).
</Warning>
