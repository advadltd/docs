You look after a group of enumerators and make sure the information they collect is correct.

## What you can do

- See which tasks your team has completed.
- **Review and approve** submitted forms, or send them back for correction. [Learn more](/fieldwork/approvals).
- Carry out **verifications** to confirm records are accurate. [Learn more](/fieldwork/verifications).
- Respond to **SOS alerts** from your team. [Learn more](/fieldwork/sos-incidents).

## A typical day

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check new submissions">
    Open the list of forms waiting for review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or send back">
    Approve good work. If something is missing or wrong, send it back with a short note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up">
    Check in with enumerators who are behind.
  </Step>
</Steps>
