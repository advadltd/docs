Supervisors oversee fieldwork within their assigned Projects. They review their team's submissions, monitor activity, collect records when assigned, and respond to safety incidents.

## What a Supervisor can do

| Area | Supervisor abilities |
| --- | --- |
| **Projects** | View assigned Projects and tasks, preview forms, and review visible task records |
| **Collection** | Collect records for tasks directly assigned to the Supervisor |
| **Submissions** | View records within the assigned Project scope, filter them, and export matching data |
| **Approvals** | Review and approve submitted Enumerator records in the Supervisor's scope |
| **Verifications** | Create, review, and confirm comparisons within assigned Projects |
| **SOS** | Receive and respond to alerts from direct-report Enumerators |
| **Analytics** | View metrics limited to the Supervisor's permitted Projects |

## Supervisor limits

A Supervisor cannot:

- Create or manage Projects, Project Types, tasks, or forms.
- Create accounts, change roles, or change reporting lines.
- Create, remove, or reinstate task assignments.
- Review or confirm verification requests outside the assigned Project scope.
- View records outside the assigned Project scope.
- Collect a task without a direct active assignment.
- Edit a finalized submission.

Contact an Admin when configuration, accounts, or assignments need to change.

## Recommended daily routine

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check SOS incidents">
    Respond immediately to any alert from a direct-report Enumerator [SOS Incidents](/roles/supervisor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review assigned Projects">
    Open [**Projects**](/fieldwork/projects-and-tasks) and check current tasks, records, and field activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process Approvals">
    Review waiting Enumerator submissions and [approve](/fieldwork/approvals) only complete, credible records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor progress">
    Use **Dashboard**, **Analytics**, and submission filters to find missing, delayed, or unusual work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up with the team">
    Resolve field questions and report account, assignment, or form problems to an Admin.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Approve an Enumerator record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Approvals">
    Select the submitted record you want to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm its identity">
    Check the Project, task, reference, collector, collection time, and form version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the answers">
    Check required and conditional answers for completeness and internal consistency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review evidence">
    Open photo attachments and check capture details, GPS coordinates, and recorded address when available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve carefully">
    Select **Approve** only when the record is acceptable and belongs to your permitted Project scope.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Approval accepts a finalized record. There is no return-for-editing action. If more work is required, ask the Enumerator to make a new submission and notify an Admin when necessary.
</Warning>

## Collect your own record

A Supervisor can collect only when an Admin has assigned the task directly to them.

1. Open **Projects** and choose the assigned Project.
2. Select **Collect** beside the assigned task.
3. Complete every stage and required evidence.
4. Save a draft if the work is incomplete.
5. Review and submit the record when finished.

Supervisor submissions enter the workflow with the authorized higher-role status defined by the app; they do not require the same Enumerator approval step.

## Review a verification

1. Open **Verifications**.
2. To create a comparison, enter the approved source Network Assessment UUID and the finalized LV Network Assessment UUID, then select **Create request**.
3. Open the request and compare the original and independent field values.
4. Confirm that both records refer to the same asset and that the evidence supports the comparison.
5. Add optional notes and select **Confirm verified** when the review is complete.

You can perform these actions only within your assigned Project scope.

## Respond to an SOS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the incident immediately">
    Review who sent it, the time, coordinates, description, Project, and task context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Acknowledge it">
    Select **Acknowledge incident** so others know the alert is being handled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Coordinate the response">
    Use **Open location in Maps** when useful and contact the person or emergency responders through the approved safety process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve it">
    After the situation is handled, enter a clear resolution note and select **Mark resolved**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The SOS feature does not replace local emergency services. If delivery is uncertain or danger is immediate, use direct emergency communication.
</Warning>

## Monitor and export work

Open **Submissions** to filter by Project, Project Type, task, status, dates, equipment type, collector ID, or search text. Select **Apply filters** before exporting. Exports include all matching records within your permitted scope, not only the current page.

Use **Analytics** to review visible submission totals, workflow statuses, activity by Project or task, and open SOS incidents.

## When to contact an Admin

Contact an Admin when:

- A team member cannot sign in or needs an account change.
- A Project, task, or form is missing or incorrect.
- An assignment is missing, expired, or should be removed.
- A published form needs to change.
- A verification belongs to a Project outside your assigned scope or needs administrative investigation.
- A record or export appears outside the expected scope.
